About

Writer, yoga teacher, and channel

Tanya Robie is a writer, yoga teacher, and channel whose work lives where creative and spiritual practice meet. Her debut memoir, Hush Little Dragon: A Magical Memoir, follows her training in telepathy through the shutdown years, a love story that returned from the other side of the veil, and the road through Mexico and Central and South America that made her a writer.

She fell in love with the power of story through Latin American literature, and spent her twenties living, studying, and working across Latin America. That decade left a lasting imprint on her perspective and continues to shape her writing. Isabel Allende and Gabriel García Márquez remain her north stars.

Tanya holds a BFA in Writing for Social Change from Prescott College and an MFA in Fiction from Spalding University, and is certified in Yoga as Muse for Creative Writing™. In 2010 she founded TanyaLoves, where she has supported writers and spiritual seekers for more than fifteen years through in-person and virtual offerings that braid spiritual, wellness, and writing practices. She began offering channeled readings in 2023 and now weaves foundational telepathy skills into her writing classes.

In 2013 she founded Rise Together, a wellness community built on outreach and education for women seeking personal, financial, and spiritual well-being. Over seven years she grew a team through social media, trained with global experts in entrepreneurship and community building, and spoke to audiences of hundreds on empowered entrepreneurship and the ways wellness practices enhance a life. She has gathered women into book groups, accountability circles, and writing groups, and keeps a trusted following of dedicated students alongside a wide personal and professional network.

She is co-author, with Darlene Van de Grift, of The Multidimensional Memoirs, a five-book series in production with Pegasus Publishing House (UK). The first volume, EarthBound: The Genesis of the Great Experiment, is expected later this year.

She lives and writes in New York’s Hudson River Valley.

Memoir

Hush Little Dragon

A Magical Memoir

When shutdown closed the world, Tanya Robie took refuge in a most unexpected place: telepathy training. To her surprise, she quickly began to hear and see beyond the veil, and the question she had chased her whole writing life, about the connection and the juice between creative and spiritual practice, suddenly illuminated. Then Mauro, a magnificent lover from her years in Mexico who died young, showed up again as a spirit, eager to continue their work together.

But to be a clear channel, to hold a high enough frequency and stay empty for transmissions, her teacher told her she would have to get over hating herself. Enough with all the self-doubt. And to be a writer, she would have to be willing to be seen.

Set on the road through Latin America, deep in the mountains of the Sierra Norte de Puebla, Mexico, and in New York’s Hudson River Valley, Hush Little Dragon is a memoir that pushes form and pulls in magic. It is the story of learning to access and co-create with telepathy in order to heal, evolve, and write. It slides between the seen and unseen realms, reveals the magic in what is real, and arrives at one knowing: self-love is the key that opens everything.

Written for writers, creatives, and spiritual seekers, the book also works as a foundational guide to using telepathy in service of your craft, your vocation, and your spiritual, physical, and emotional well-being.

Community Write

One of the biggest challenges we face in writing is showing up to the page. Community and deadlines (lifelines) can really help. Why not let yourself be held accountable, and in good company, while you write your book, short stories, essays, poems, or screenplay?

Community Write is the opportunity to show up to the page twice a week, in community. Every Tuesday and Friday, Tanya holds space for participants to gather on Zoom and write quietly, together. These power hours open with a very brief connection, then everyone mutes and writes.

“Developing our imagination, the language of the soul, allows Spirit to work through us as we answer our calling to the page.” Linda Naiman

What if your imagination is a lot more powerful than you’ve allowed yourself to believe? What if a more fluid connection to Creativity could bring you more joy and creative productivity? What if you allowed writing to support your healing and evolution?

Writing: Magical Connection to Your Divine Muse

You’ve felt it before, right? The rush. Those magical moments immersed in the creative when time stops and the world falls away and it’s just you and the page and the piece you are creating. What is that? The energy behind inspiration? The words that come gushing when we meet the muse and merge with creativity? What is writer’s flow, and how can we get more of it?

Yes, Tanya believes so. For years she has explored the intersection between spirituality and creativity. The deeper she goes, the more her writing life opens and expands. Yours can too.

Each class offers guided meditation, experiential learning, lecture, writing exercises, and time to share. Open to writers of all mediums. While elements of craft are taught, this course focuses on creative practice: how to show up with the whole you and be met there at the page. Community Write on Tuesdays and Fridays is included and optional.
