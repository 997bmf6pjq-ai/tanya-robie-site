About Tanya

Tanya Robie is a writer, yoga teacher, and channel whose work lives where creative and spiritual practice meet. She fell in love with the power of story through Latin American literature and spent her twenties living, studying, and working across Latin America, a decade that still shapes her writing.

She holds an MFA in Fiction from Spalding University and a BFA in Writing for Social Change from Prescott College. In 2010 she founded TanyaLoves, and in 2013 Rise Together, a wellness community for women. For seventeen years she has taught yoga, meditation, and writing as a spiritual practice, and since 2023 she has offered channeled readings. She is co-author of The Multidimensional Memoirs, in production with Pegasus Publishing House (UK).

Memoir

Hush Little Dragon

A Magical Memoir

When shutdown closed the world, Tanya Robie took refuge in a most unexpected place: telepathy training. To her surprise, she quickly began to hear and see beyond the veil, and the question she had chased her whole writing life, about the connection and the juice between creative and spiritual practice, suddenly illuminated. Then Mauro, a magnificent lover from her years in Mexico who died young, showed up again as a spirit, eager to continue their work together.

But to be a clear channel, to hold a high enough frequency and stay empty for transmissions, her teacher told her she would have to get over hating herself. Enough with all the self-doubt. And to be a writer, she would have to be willing to be seen.

Set on the road through Latin America, deep in the mountains of the Sierra Norte de Puebla, Mexico, and in New York’s Hudson River Valley, Hush Little Dragon is a memoir that pushes form and pulls in magic. It is the story of learning to access and co-create with telepathy in order to heal, evolve, and write. It slides between the seen and unseen realms, reveals the magic in what is real, and arrives at one knowing: self-love is the key that opens everything.

Written for writers, creatives, and spiritual seekers, the book also works as a foundational guide to using telepathy in service of your craft, your vocation, and your spiritual, physical, and emotional well-being.

Community Write

One of the biggest challenges we face in writing is showing up to the page. Community and deadlines (lifelines) can really help. Why not let yourself be held accountable, and in good company, while you write your book, short stories, essays, poems, or screenplay?

Community Write is the opportunity to show up to the page twice a week, in community. Every Tuesday and Friday, Tanya holds space for participants to gather on Zoom and write quietly, together. These power hours open with a very brief connection, then everyone mutes and writes.
